Sankebetsu Brown Bear Incident

This is one of the most tragic and famous bear attacks in Japanese history, which took place in December 1915 in Sankebetsu, Hokkaido. A brown bear that failed to hibernate, driven by hunger and fear, wandered into a small village called Sankebetsu. Over several terrible days, it attacked homes and took the lives of many innocent villagers, including women and children. The event became a lasting symbol of tragedy — not because the bear was evil, but because nature, when disturbed or misunderstood, can turn from harmony to horror.
